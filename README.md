# GTA: Vice City — Ethiopia Edition (`san-andreas-ethiopia`)

A browser and mobile-friendly 3D open-world action game built with Three.js and WebGL, blending classic Vice City neon systems and an interactive in-game smartphone with iconic Ethiopian cities and highways.

## Featured Cities & Landmarks
- **Addis Ababa:** Meskel Square monument & Ethiopian flag plaza, Bole Neon Strip, Merkato Market, and Entoto Mountains.
- **Adama:** Rift Valley palm boulevard.
- **Bahir Dar & Lake Tana:** Lakeside marina and amphibious `SEAWAYS` lake crossings.
- **Hawassa:** Lake Hawassa waterfront strip.
- **Dire Dawa & Harar:** Historic Harar Jugol & Dire Dawa district.

## Vehicles
- **Infernus Supercar** & **Cheetah V8** (80s neon exotics)
- **Blue-White Minibus** & **Bajaj Tuk-Tuk** (Ethiopian transit)
- **Vice Police Cruiser**, **Hunter Helicopter**, **Rhino Tank**, & **Fighter Jet**

## Controls
- **Mobile / Tablet:** Left virtual thumbstick to move/steer; on-screen buttons for **Enter/Exit Car**, **Jump/Fly**, **Sprint/Turbo**, **Attack/Fire**, **Phone**, **Radio**, and **Weapon**.
- **PC Keyboard:** `W A S D` or Arrow keys move/drive, `Shift` sprint/turbo, `Space` jump/fly, `F` enter/exit vehicle, `Q` switch weapon, `P` open smartphone, `R` cycle radio (`Flash FM` / `Fever 105`), `Click` attack/fire.
